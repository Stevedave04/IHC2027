About the Conference

Ireland's premier gathering for hypnotherapy excellence

Since its founding, the Irish Hypnotherapy Conference has become the country's most respected platform for professional development, peer connection and advancing the science and art of hypnotherapy. Each year we bring together Ireland's finest practitioners alongside internationally renowned experts.

The 2027 conference centres on Empowering Connections, Advancing Expertise, Embracing Clarity, our most ambitious programme to date.

Group bookings are welcome. For parties of 5 or more, please contact us directly at john@irishhypnotherapyconference.ie to discuss pricing and logistics.

Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the conference. A 50% refund applies between 14 and 30 days prior. No refunds are issued within 14 days of the start date. Tickets are fully transferable at no extra charge. See our Refund Policy for full details.

3-Day Pass holders receive 90-day access to session recordings. Platinum VIP ticket holders receive lifetime access. Day Pass holders do not receive recordings.

The Saturday evening Gala Dinner and Awards Ceremony is included in the 3-Day Pass and Platinum VIP ticket. Day Pass holders may purchase a Gala Dinner add-on separately — contact us for availability.
