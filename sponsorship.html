Who Attends IHC

IHC delegates are practising professionals — not students browsing for free content. They invest in their practice and are actively looking for quality tools, training, and services. Our 2026 audience included:

  • Independent hypnotherapy practitioners (sole traders and small clinics)
  • Hypnotherapy trainers and school owners
  • Psychologists and counsellors integrating hypnotherapy
  • Healthcare professionals with a hypnotherapy specialism
  • Students in final year of accredited training programmes
  • Delegates from across Ireland, the UK, and continental Europe
